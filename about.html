About Chhabra Lawyers & Associates

Chhabra Lawyers & Associates provides reliable legal advice and representation for individuals and families. The firm handles matters before the District Court, Hanumangarh and the Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur, with a focus on clear guidance and responsible support for every client.

Advocate Rajesh Chhabra, M.A., LL.B., LL.M., has been practising at the District Court, Hanumangarh since 1996. With long-standing experience, he provides practical legal support in criminal cases, family matters, NDPS cases, POCSO matters, and SC/ST matters.

Advocate Aman Chhabra, B.Com., LL.B., LL.M., has been practising before the Rajasthan High Court at Jodhpur since 2024. He offers clear and dependable legal assistance in criminal DB matters, protection matters, bail applications, habeas corpus matters, civil writ petitions, and other criminal cases.
